A little preparation goes a long way

Before you go

Passports & documents

Bring your valid passport and check the entry requirements for your nationality. As a conservative precaution, we recommend passport validity for at least six months beyond your October 28 return date (through April 28, 2027).

Keep passports and travel documents in your carry-on.

Check in before travel

Complete Beaches online check-in using your own reservation information through the official Sandals/Beaches app or check-in portal.

Check in for United flights when airline check-in opens, typically 24 hours before departure, and plan to arrive at Newark about three hours before your international flight.

Optional airport Fast Track

Arrival Fast Track assistance at Providenciales may make the airport arrival process more convenient, especially on busy weekends.

$90 per person, including taxes and fees, based on the quoted rate. Optional, subject to availability; this does not include transportation.

Departure Fast Track & lounge

Optional departure Fast Track and VIP lounge services may be available at Providenciales Airport. If you’d like details or would like me to check availability and pricing, please let me know before travel.

Airport tip: If the main departure waiting area feels crowded, you can try heading upstairs and walking toward the back to look for additional seating, where accessible. Seating is not guaranteed.

Travel protection

Consider reviewing your travel insurance coverage before departure. Depending on policy terms, travel protection may help with certain unexpected delays, interruptions or medical situations.

If you’d like to revisit your options, please reach out to Kariann. Coverage, eligibility and purchase deadlines vary.
